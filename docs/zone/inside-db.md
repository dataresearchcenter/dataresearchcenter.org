# Register Your Interest in the<br>Inside Deutsche Bank Archive

On behalf of the consortium, we are gathering interest in future journalistic work with the Inside Deutsche Bank source data. Please read the information on this page carefully.
</div>

## What you need to know before

There will be a cool-down period of multiple weeks after the initial project release. After that, access to the information is restricted to vetted journalists. Every request will be manually checked. For this, we need the following information from you:

1. **Who you are:** Your full name and current role.
2. **Where you work:** The name of your employer. If you freelance, name drop 1-2 recent gigs.
3. **Social media or website:** A link to your LinkedIn, your profile page on your employers website, or similar.
4. Optional: **Why you're interested:** Do you have story leads in mind?
5. Optional: **Who can vouch**: Name a journalist on the project who has worked with you in the past.

Please refrain from sending incomplete requests that do not answer the questions above, and please do not email us if you are not a journalist.

## How to Register Your Interest

From your work email address, please send an email to [inside-db@dataresearchcenter.org](mailto:inside-db@dataresearchcenter.org) with the subject line: **Expression of Interest - [Your Name]**

Emails that do not use this subject line format will be discarded automatically, as will multiple requests from the same email address.

## The fine print

Submitting an expression of interest does not guarantee access. We will review submissions and may reach out with follow-up questions.

By submitting your expression of interest via email, you acknowledge and agree that we may store and process the information you provide for this purpose.

If you are a journalist and have questions about the published stories or would like to interview members of the consortium, please email [press@inside-db.com](mailto:press@inside-db.com) directly.
