## What we do

At DARC, we provide expert-driven, flexible research and data solutions tailored to the unique needs of journalists, NGOs, civil society organizations, and select corporate clients.

Powered by [OpenAleph](openaleph.md), our versatile open-source search tool, we ensure fast, efficient, and precise investigations, delivering the reliability of an in-house team with the convenience of a monthly subscription.

### Comprehensive Research

A global team with local expertise, we deliver context-driven solutions.

- Tracking assets – real estate, ships and planes, corporate record retrieval
- Locating people, contacts and social networks
- Acquiring and analyzing import/export data
- Investigating website ownership and affiliations
- Geolocating images and videos

### Managed OpenAleph

Our open source tool [OpenAleph](openaleph.md), the software behind [darc.zone](https://darc.zone), simplifies large-scale document and data search.

- Setting up and managing your organization's own OpenAleph infrastructure
- Securing access to reference datasets
- Developing custom OpenAleph features tailored to your team’s needs
- Providing technical support, onboarding and training

### Data & Tooling

We build the technology you need to get the most out of leaked data and scraped information.

- Data cleaning, analysis and interpretation
- Sourcing, requesting and scraping hard-to-find datasets
- Offering easy and secure ways to store and share large amounts of data

### Project Management & Collaboration Support

We bring decades of experience managing cross-border, cross-newsroom investigations.

- Equipping you with tools for knowledge and project management
- Sharing real-world insights from complex collaborative investigations
- Consulting on best practices for sensitive communication and security

### Trainings, Workshops & Events

Below are some of the hands-on training sessions and workshops that we've run in the past.

- OSINT techniques: crypto currency tracing, tracking ships and planes, finding people with minimal clues
- Data techniques: using AI in investigations, command line for journalists, pulling data from documents
- Hackathons for 30+ people focused on building investigative tooling
- Multi-week, in-depth research and data fellowships

[Get in touch](mailto:hi@dataresearchcenter.org){.btn .btn--primary}

## Who we are

DARC is an independent organization providing bespoke data and research services for investigative teams.

Our team has decades of combined experience in investigative journalism and developing research tools and software. Our founders Karina, Simon and Jan have worked on multiple award-winning, cross-border, cross-newsroom investigations with [Norddeutscher Rundfunk](https://ndr.de), the [Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project](https://occrp.org) and [CORRECTIV](https://correctiv.org).

With our core team and trusted network of collaborators, we can easily scale to meet the demands of any project.
